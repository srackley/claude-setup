**URL:** https://github.com/wanderu/canopy/pull/448
**Author:** meag-wanderu
**Classification:** NEEDS_REVIEW
**Review action:** COMMENT

### Summary

Adds 11 lines to `.claude/rules/planning.md`: a "Writing issues" section with guidance on what epic bodies should contain and four anti-patterns to avoid. Documentation-only PR; CI passing.

**What it does:** Gives teams explicit guidance not to duplicate sub-issue status in parent epic bodies.
**Why:** Issue #447 — maintaining status tables and sub-issue summaries in parent issues creates drift and dual-maintenance burden.

---

### SUGGESTIONS (1)

**`.claude/rules/planning.md` — "Writing issues" section**

The PR's own acceptance criteria (from issue #447) require guidance on three things: parent issues, sub-issues, and anti-patterns. The PR delivers parent issue guidance and anti-patterns, but contains no positive guidance on what belongs in a *sub-issue* body. The challenger confirmed: no sub-issue body guidance exists anywhere in `.claude/rules/` — not in `planning.md`, not in any other rules file. A developer writing a sub-issue for the first time has no guidance here on what to include (acceptance criteria, link to parent, scope boundary, definition of done, spec pointers).

**Suggested addition:** A `### Sub-issue bodies` subsection (or brief paragraph) specifying what goes in a sub-issue: the unit of work it accomplishes, acceptance criteria, a link to the parent epic, relevant spec sections, and any dependencies on other sub-issues.

Note: The section heading `## Writing issues` implies broader coverage than the current content delivers; this resolves once sub-issue guidance is added.

**Evidence:** Challenger grepped all files in `.claude/rules/` — zero results for sub-issue body guidance outside the anti-patterns list.
